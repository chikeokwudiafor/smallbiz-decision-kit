Small Business Decision Kit

This kit reads the sales export your shop already has and answers four questions in plain English. It also checks the file first, so you know which numbers you can trust.

Your file is read inside this browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

Your sales file

You can also drag a file here. Square, Shopify, Stripe, Toast and most spreadsheets can export one. The kit expects one row per order.

    Who is drifting away?

    Regular customers who have gone quiet

    A regular here is someone with at least three orders. They count as drifting when the time since their last order is more than double their own usual gap, at least 30 days, and under six months. The copied list pastes straight into a spreadsheet.

    Ask an AI about these numbers

    Build a privacy-safe prompt

    The kit writes a prompt you can paste into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or any AI chat. It holds totals and averages only, and it tells the AI to stick to them.
